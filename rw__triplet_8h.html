Detailed Description

The stages of the Random Window (RW) search selected by rw_opt (see RW_OPT_T3 etc. in util_io.h and doc/rw_triplet_degeneracies.tex): the weight-3 degeneracies T_3 of (H, L) (Stage 1), their contraction and the known generators G (Stage 2), the canonical classes of the minimum-weight codewords (Stage 5), and the expansion of the collected codewords by T_3 (rw_opt bit 32).

A weight-3 degeneracy (a triplet) is a set {u, v, w} of three nonzero columns of H with h_u + h_v + h_w = 0 and l_u + l_v + l_w = 0 (the columns of L): adding it to a codeword does not change its syndromes, e.g., a CNOT hook triangle of a circuit DEM. The triplets are found exactly by Lemma 1 of the notes: the smallest row of the union of the three columns is in exactly two of them, which have this row as their smallest row, so that a lookup of the Zobrist hash of h_u + h_v (and l_u + l_v) for each pair {u, v} of columns with the same smallest row finds every triplet once. They give the pair table (u, v) -> w used by the contraction of the candidate codewords (2 -> 1: {u, v} -> {w}; 3 -> 0), by the canonical form of a codeword (2 <-> 2 exchanges across two triplets with a common column), and by the expansion of the collected codewords.

Enumerate the weight-3 degeneracies of (H, L) (Lemma 1 of doc/rw_triplet_degeneracies.tex).

The columns are those of H (rows of HT, sorted) and the column masks of L (NULL for a classical code: then no triplets are enumerated, as a weight-3 vector with zero syndrome is a codeword). Zero columns of H are skipped (the pure-logical ones are listed in pure). If the enumeration needs more than budget hash probes, it is skipped (complete = 0). The tables are freed (and the counts kept) if there are no triplets.

Parameters
HTThe transpose of H (n rows: the rows of H in each column of H, sorted).
mThe number of rows of H.
maskLThe column masks of L (NULL: classical code).
budgetThe largest number of hash probes.
Returns
The new structure (free with rw_t3_free()).
